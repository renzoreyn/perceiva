money never feels like itself, does it

4,700 AMD looks like you bought air. gone instantly. forgettable.
but then you translate it and it turns into 170,000 IDR and suddenly it has weight. it becomes real. a coffee turns into something you actually think about.

that gap. that mental lag. that’s what this thing is trying to kill.

perceiva is a finance tracker built on one annoying truth
your brain does not understand money across borders. it just pretends it does.

so instead of letting you sit there doing fake math in your head like it means something, it just shows you. instantly. brutally. across every currency you touch.

you log a transaction and it doesn’t just nod politely at your number
it translates it. in real time. into everything else your life is quietly tied to.

no spreadsheets. no “wait what was the rate again.” no mental gymnastics you’ll forget tomorrow anyway.

just clarity. the kind that doesn’t ask permission.

8 currencies. live rates. one dashboard.

USD · GBP · EUR · CHF · CNY · IDR · AMD · RUB

built for the people living in currency overlap. salary here, rent there, savings somewhere else you only check when you’re brave.

and yeah. it’s not trying to feel like finance software.
it’s trying to feel like finally understanding what your money is actually doing while you’re not looking.

…don’t get used to it though. money still sucks.
